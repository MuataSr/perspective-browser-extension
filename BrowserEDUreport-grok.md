## 1. Current Landscape of Educational Browser Extensions

The edtech extension scene is thriving but still feels like a patchwork quilt—cozy in spots, threadbare in others. Tools aim to amp up productivity, spark engagement, and turn browsers into virtual classrooms for teachers and students alike. Most rock the freemium vibe: free basics to lure you in, premium perks for the committed.

### 1.1. Writing and Grammar Assistance

This category's the old reliable, with AI now supercharging real-time edits.

* Grammarly: Still the boss, catching grammar gaffes, style slips, and now AI-powered tone tweaks and plagiarism scans in premium.
* ProWritingAid: Grammarly's scrappy rival, offering deep-dive reports, thesauri, and checks for overused words.
* Jenni AI: A fresh entrant for 2025, specializing in academic writing with AI suggestions for structure and citations.

### 1.2. Multimedia Creation and Interaction

These turn passive scrolling into interactive masterpieces, perfect for flipped classrooms.

* Loom & Screencastify: Screen-recording staples for videos, now with AI auto-captions and highlight reels.
* Kami: PDF annotator extraordinaire, adding collaborative edits and voice notes.
* Edpuzzle: Embeds quizzes in videos from YouTube et al., with AI suggesting question spots.
* Brisk Teaching: New AI kid on the block, generating lesson plans and quizzes right in your browser.
* Scripsy AI: Summarizes YouTube lectures on the fly.

### 1.3. Organization and Resource Management

Wrangling web chaos into neat piles.

* Google Classroom: Hub for assignments, integrating with Drive.
* Pocket & Notion Web Clipper: Save articles for later, with AI tagging in newer versions.
* Smallpdf: PDF Swiss Army knife for editing and compressing.
* Fireflies AI: Auto-transcribes meetings and searches notes.

### 1.4. Specialized Educational Functions

Niche tools for specific brain-teasers.

* EquatIO: Math equation builder for STEM.
* Read Aloud: Text-to-speech for accessibility.
* InsertLearning: Turns webpages into lessons with embedded questions.
* MyBib: Citation generator for MLA/APA.
* SchoolAI: Browser extension for student-safe AI tools anywhere.

These prove demand for seamless browser integrations, but 2025's AI wave is pushing boundaries further.
